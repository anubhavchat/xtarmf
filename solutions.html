Xtartt is built for that. It's a quick engagement you walk away with a working setup and a clear first-steps roadmap. Low cost. High clarity.

Xtartt is fixed price (₹999). Xmooth, Xtra, and Xperience are scoped after the first call. We don't quote fixed rates blind different companies have different problems. The scoping call is free and non-binding.
What about AI?
AI ships as a feature inside an engagement where it makes sense not as a separate pitch. A client review generator, compliance assistant, or predictive model. Shipped and real, not theoretical.
Do you sign NDAs?
Always. NDA first, before any conversation. Client information stays private. We don't use engagements as marketing material without permission.
How fast can we start?
First call typically happens within a week. Diagnostics and audits usually start within 2-3 weeks. Build engagements take longer depending on our capacity we're transparent about that from day one.
