SECTION / 02

How Xtartmf is structured.

Built for depth, not scale. The structure exists to protect client quality senior practitioners directly involved in every engagement, every time.

STRUCTURE 01

Senior-led delivery

Every engagement from a 1-week IFA diagnostic to a 10-week AI build is led and executed by the senior practitioner. The Firm does not sub-contract or delegate to junior analysts.

STRUCTURE 02

Strict capacity limits

Fractional advisory is structurally capped. Deep engagements are taken sequentially, not concurrently. That's a hard limit, not a marketing line.

STRUCTURE 03

Conflict-free by design

The Firm does not hold retainers with competing platforms in the same segment. All potential conflicts are surfaced and resolved before scoping begins.

STRUCTURE 04

NDA-first, always

Every engagement begins with an NDA before any privileged information is shared. The Firm does not maintain a public client list or use client work as portfolio material without explicit consent.
