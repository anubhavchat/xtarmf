SECTION / 02

Operating principles.

A few rules we don't bend on for the client's benefit as much as ours.

i.

Senior-led, not delegated

Every artefact PRD, SQL query, code, audit is produced by the senior practitioner. Clients pay for senior judgment delivered directly, not for an org chart.

ii.

Bounded engagements

Every project has a defined scope, timeline, and outcome. The Firm does not take retainers without an underlying delivery thesis.

iii.

Capacity is protected

Fractional advisory is capped to protect depth. Deep engagements are taken one at a time that's a structural rule, not a goal.

iv.

No conflict, ever

The Firm does not work simultaneously with directly competing platforms in the same segment. Conflicts are declared and declined before scoping begins.

v.

Confidentiality by default

NDA-first engagements. Client names, problems, and outcomes are not used as marketing material without explicit consent.

vi.

Concrete outcomes, not opinions

Every engagement closes with something shipped an audit, a roadmap, a working feature, a hand-off document. Not a slide deck.
