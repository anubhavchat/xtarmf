How we think about MF distribution

SECTION / 01

Built for the full distribution spectrum from a single IFA to a listed brokerage.

Most consultants serve one segment. XtARmf is built to serve four because the core problem (reliable, compliant, scalable MF distribution) is the same at every size. Only the complexity changes.

Tier 01

IFAs & Small Distributors

Running a practice on spreadsheets and WhatsApp. We remove 8+ hours/week of ops work onboarding people, reconciling money, prepping client meetings without forcing you to buy a system you don't need.

Works if
You're spending more time on admin than on clients. Want clean plumbing. Prefer lean over fancy.
Tier 02

Mid-size Distributors & RIA Platforms

You've outgrown spreadsheets but can't justify a full product team. We fix what's breaking: commission flows, onboarding friction, compliance gaps, client servicing bottlenecks. Then we help you scale without rebuilding.

Works if
You've built something that works, but it doesn't yet scale cleanly. Need an operator's judgment on what to fix vs. build vs. skip.
Tier 03

Brokerages, AMCs & Wealth Platforms

Integrating with BSE StAR MF, NSE MF, RTAs at scale. We know where it breaks mandate flows, NAV cutoffs, UCC architecture, SLA negotiation because we've been inside the platform. We help you design infrastructure that doesn't crumble at 50K transactions/day.

Works if
Your MF or distribution stack is infrastructure-grade. Decisions ripple across 10+ teams. Downtime costs money.
Tier 04

Founders Building Wealthtech

Running a B2C investing app, AI advisory layer, or distributor SaaS. You need product architecture, distribution strategy, and regulatory positioning from someone who's been inside the infrastructure you're building on. That's us.

Works if
You're pre-Series A or early-stage. Need an operator's perspective, not a consultant's framework.
